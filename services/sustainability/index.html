Sustainability & Green Building

Designing homes and shops with enduring quality, reduced utility overhead, and healthy indoor air quality.

Core Principles of Sustainable Design

At its core, sustainability means the capacity to endure. When applied to architectural design, it encompasses material selection, site development, building life cycle, and recurring energy consumption.

  • Site Solar Orientation: Maximizing natural passive heat gains in winter while shading hot summer rays.
  • Centrally Located Utilities: Shortening plumbing runs and placing HVAC in conditioned space.
  • Advanced Air Envelope Sealing: Preventing moisture penetration and drafts.
  • Low-VOC Finishes: Ensuring clean, healthy indoor air quality for your family.
