Structural Insulated Panels

Structural Insulated Panels or SIP’s, outperform conventional building methods in almost every way. SIP’s are high performance building panels comprised of a core of rigid foam insulation between two layers of oriented strand board (OSB). These panels are manufactured under factory controlled conditions according to your specific architectural plans. Consider some reasons why SIP’s may be the right choice for you:

Comfort & Energy Efficiency

A 6 ½” SIP outperforms a conventional 2×6 wall which has less than a R-21 insulation value. Fiberglass insulation in a conventional wall sags overtime and does not fit perfectly in wall, floor, or ceiling cavities or around plumbing and electrical components. A 2×6 wall will generally yield a maximum R-18 if properly installed. The rigid foam insulation in a SIP however, is composed of 98 percent air, therefore preventing all air drafts and completely eliminating thermal bridging. SIP wall cores eliminate convection and condensation creating a permeability of less than 1.0 perm and can achieve R values between R-16 and R57 depending on the foam and thickness. This reduces the temperature peaks that conventional homes experience in the summer and winter months. A SIP building envelope can decrease heating and cooling costs by fifty percent. Consequently, HVAC systems can be reduced. While the initial construction costs are greater than conventional construction methods, the return on investment is usually within 3-5 years.

Less Waste & Time

Because the panels are manufactured in a factory setting there is significantly less jobsite waste and environmental impact. SIP buildings save a significant amount of time because larger, stronger walls, floors, and roof sections can be craned into place in a matter of days thus creating a ‘dryed in’ environment much quicker than framed structures.

Strength & Quality

The structural characteristics of SIP’s are similar to steel I beams. The OSB sheathing functions like the flange while the rigid foam core creates the web. This gives the panel exceptional strength enabling these structures to resist seismic activity and high winds. SIP’s conform to the requirements of the International Building Code (IBC) and the International Residential Code (IRC) and are manufactured under quality controls. SIP’s have a Class 1 Fire Resistance Rating – the best available for combustible materials. For more information please visit www.sips.org.
