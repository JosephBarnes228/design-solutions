Custom Home Plans - The Design Process

Transforming your ideas, lifestyle requirements, and site conditions into engineered construction blueprints.

How Do I Get Started?

Clients often come to our office with a decade of magazine clippings or internet articles in an effort to communicate what type of home they wish to build. Other clients have little more than a wish list. Our goal is to assemble critical information such as budgetary constraints, site restrictions, architectural style, setting, and the overarching theme of the home.

How Will I Be Involved?

We invite client participation and interaction! We begin by creating initial floor plan and elevation concepts, moving fluidly toward refined detailed CAD drawings over the course of design meetings. We strongly recommend meeting with prospective contractors during this phase to maintain budget alignment.

Will I Need Structural Engineering?

During our consultation, we will discuss whether structural engineering is required. Large vaulted wall sections or extensive window walls often require engineering oversight. Good engineering simplifies construction and saves money in the field. Design Solutions maintains trusted relationships with local structural engineers to ensure a seamless process.
