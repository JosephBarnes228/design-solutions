Site Design & Topography Planning

Integrating building orientation with slope, view corridors, microclimates, and lot setbacks.

Why Site-Based Design Matters

Purchasing a generic stock plan without regard for the physical site often results in missed view opportunities, improper sun orientation, or costly foundation grading errors. Topography, view corridors, drainage patterns, habitat buffers, and setback constraints dictate optimal room placement.

We analyze hillside slopes, natural light angles, and surrounding neighborhood scale to craft custom homes and shop structures that feel natively anchored to the land.
