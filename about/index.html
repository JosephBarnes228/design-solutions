About Design Solutions

Dedicated to creating sustainable, energy-efficient, and functional home & shop designs across the Pacific Northwest for over two decades.

Rob Barnes - Principal Designer

Design Solutions is a Vancouver-based residential design firm focusing on providing quality home and shop designs specifically crafted for our clients' individual needs, finances, and building site. We take a synergistic approach when combining floor plans, exterior facades, site planning, material conservation, and resale value.

Serving the Pacific Northwest for over twenty years, we specialize in developing custom homes and outbuildings that work in harmony with their physical environments. Each project presents a one-of-a-kind opportunity to address a customer's goals while creating enduring, timeless, and responsible architecture.

It is important that every customer feels heard and has a clear understanding of the design process and timeline. We work with homeowners as well as contractors and enjoy educating customers regarding current trends, cost-saving measures, aging-in-place options, energy efficiency, and sustainability.
