Aging in Place & Universal Design

Every day, thousands of people across America retire. This group possesses unique considerations when purchasing or building a new custom home. Universal design ensures your home remains accessible, safe, and comfortable for decades to come.

  • Main Floor Living: Everyday activities based on the ground level with zero-threshold entries.
  • Manageable Square Footage: Less costly to purchase, heat, clean, and maintain.
  • Open Floor Plans: Spacious flow for entertaining without wasteful hallways.
  • Curbless Showers: Zero-threshold walk-in showers and wide doorways meeting ADA guidelines tastefully.

Energy Efficiency Starts in the Design Phase

Energy-efficient, sustainable homes aren't adapted on the job site with insulation upgrades or Energy Star appliances. The critical time to consider energy efficiency, thermal comfort, and sustainability begins at the drafting phase.

Site orientation, window placement relative to sun angles, passive solar eave overhangs, HVAC mechanical room locations, and compact floor plan envelopes drastically reduce heating and cooling costs over the life of the home.
